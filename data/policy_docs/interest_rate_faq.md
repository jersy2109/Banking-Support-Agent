# Interest Rate FAQ

*JBank — fictional institution, synthetic data only. Rates below are illustrative and not based on any real institution's current rates.*

## Savings Account Rates

| Account | Annual Percentage Yield (APY) |
|---|---|
| Standard Savings | 0.50% |
| High-Yield Savings | 4.25% |

Rates are variable and may change at any time without prior notice, consistent with standard industry practice for variable-rate accounts. Customers are notified of rate changes through their monthly statement.

## How Interest Is Calculated

Interest on savings accounts is calculated daily using the average daily balance method and credited to the account monthly.

## Checking Account Interest

Essential Checking does not earn interest. Premier Checking earns 0.10% APY on balances above $2,500.

## Certificates of Deposit (CDs)

| Term | APY |
|---|---|
| 6 months | 2.00% |
| 12 months | 3.50% |
| 24 months | 3.75% |
| 60 months | 4.00% |

Early withdrawal from a CD before maturity incurs a penalty equal to 90 days of interest for terms under 12 months, or 180 days of interest for terms of 12 months or longer.

## Overdraft Line of Credit Interest

If enrolled in Overdraft Line of Credit protection (see the Overdraft Policy document), interest accrues on the drawn balance at a variable APR currently set at 18.00%, calculated daily on the outstanding balance.
