# Dispute & Chargeback Process

*JBank — fictional institution, synthetic data only.*

## When to File a Dispute

Customers can file a transaction dispute if they believe a charge is:
- Unauthorized (they did not make or approve the transaction)
- Incorrect in amount (billed for more than agreed)
- For goods or services that were never received
- A duplicate charge
- The result of a merchant error (wrong item, defective item not resolved by the merchant)

## How to File

1. The customer identifies the specific transaction (date, amount, merchant name).
2. The customer confirms whether they have already attempted to resolve the issue directly with the merchant. This is not required but is noted in the case file.
3. A dispute case is opened and the customer receives a case number.

## Timelines

- Disputes must be filed within 60 days of the transaction's statement date to be eligible under standard policy.
- A provisional credit for the disputed amount is typically issued within 10 business days of the case being opened, pending investigation.
- The investigation itself may take up to 90 days for complex cases (e.g., international merchants, services not rendered).

## Required Identity Verification

Before any dispute case can be opened or discussed, the customer's identity must be verified. This is a mandatory step and cannot be skipped regardless of how routine the dispute appears.

## Outcomes

- **Resolved in customer's favor**: the provisional credit becomes permanent.
- **Resolved in merchant's favor**: the provisional credit is reversed, and the customer is notified with an explanation.
- **Inconclusive**: the case may be escalated to a human specialist for manual review; this is not something the automated system can resolve on its own.

## What the Automated System Can and Cannot Do

The automated system can open a dispute case, provide a case number, and share the case status. It cannot determine the outcome of a dispute, issue a final credit decision, or override a provisional credit. Any request to do so should be escalated to a human representative.
