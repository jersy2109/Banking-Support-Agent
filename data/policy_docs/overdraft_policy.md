# Overdraft Policy

*JBank — fictional institution, synthetic data only.*

## What Counts as an Overdraft

An overdraft occurs when a transaction is authorized that brings an account balance below $0. This can happen through debit card purchases, ATM withdrawals, checks, or automatic payments.

## Overdraft Protection Options

Customers may enroll in one of the following, but are not enrolled by default:

1. **Linked Account Transfer** — automatically transfers funds from a linked savings account to cover the shortfall. A $5 transfer fee applies per occurrence.
2. **Overdraft Line of Credit** — a pre-approved credit line (subject to a separate credit check) that covers the shortfall and accrues interest on the drawn amount at the account's standard APR.
3. **No Protection (Decline)** — transactions that would overdraft the account are declined at the point of sale or ATM with no fee charged.

## Overdraft Fees (When Not Enrolled in Protection)

- A standard overdraft fee of $34 applies per item that is paid into overdraft, up to a maximum of 3 fees per business day ($102 maximum).
- No overdraft fee is charged if the account is overdrawn by less than $5 at the end of the business day.
- An extended overdraft fee of $15 may apply if the account remains overdrawn for more than 5 consecutive business days.

## Grace Period

Customers have until 11:59 PM on the business day following an overdraft to deposit sufficient funds and avoid the standard overdraft fee, provided this is the customer's first overdraft occurrence in the preceding 12 months.

## Disputing an Overdraft Fee

Customers may request a one-time courtesy reversal of an overdraft fee if they have not received a reversal in the past 12 months. Repeated reversal requests are reviewed on a case-by-case basis and may require escalation to a human representative.
