# Account Types & Fees

*JBank — fictional institution, synthetic data only.*

## Checking Accounts

**Essential Checking**
- Monthly maintenance fee: $5, waived with a minimum daily balance of $500 or one direct deposit per month.
- No minimum opening deposit.
- Includes a debit card and unlimited electronic transfers.

**Premier Checking**
- Monthly maintenance fee: $15, waived with a minimum daily balance of $5,000.
- Includes free checks, no foreign transaction fees, and priority phone support.

## Savings Accounts

**Standard Savings**
- Monthly maintenance fee: $3, waived with a minimum daily balance of $300.
- Interest rate: see the Interest Rate FAQ document.
- Limited to 6 withdrawals per statement cycle; a $5 fee applies per withdrawal beyond that limit.

**High-Yield Savings**
- No monthly fee.
- Requires a minimum opening deposit of $1,000.
- Higher interest rate than Standard Savings; see the Interest Rate FAQ document.

## Common Fees

| Fee Type | Amount |
|---|---|
| Out-of-network ATM withdrawal | $3.00 per transaction |
| Wire transfer (domestic, outgoing) | $25.00 |
| Wire transfer (international, outgoing) | $45.00 |
| Paper statement | $2.00 per month |
| Account closure within 90 days of opening | $25.00 |
| Returned deposited item | $12.00 |

## Waivers

Monthly maintenance fees are automatically waived for customers who are active-duty military, full-time students under 24, or enrolled in a Premier relationship bundle. Waivers are applied automatically when eligibility is on file; no action is required from the customer.
