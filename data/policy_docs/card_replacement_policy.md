# Card Replacement Policy

*JBank — fictional institution, synthetic data only.*

## Reasons for Replacement

A card may be replaced for any of the following reasons:
- Lost or stolen
- Damaged (chip, magnetic stripe, or physical damage affecting use)
- Suspected fraud or compromise
- Expiration
- Name change on the account

## Immediate Actions for Lost or Stolen Cards

When a card is reported lost or stolen, it is immediately frozen (blocked from all new transactions) before any replacement is processed. This freeze cannot be reversed by the customer directly — a new card must be issued. Any pending transactions authorized before the freeze will still settle normally.

## Replacement Timelines and Costs

| Reason | Fee | Standard Delivery |
|---|---|---|
| Lost or stolen | No fee (first occurrence in 12 months) | 5–7 business days |
| Lost or stolen (repeat, same 12-month period) | $10 | 5–7 business days |
| Damaged | No fee | 5–7 business days |
| Suspected fraud | No fee | 5–7 business days |
| Expired | No fee (automatic) | Arrives before expiration date |
| Expedited delivery (any reason) | $25 | 1–2 business days |

## Identity Verification Requirement

Identity verification is required before freezing a card or issuing a replacement. This applies even when the customer is reporting their own card lost — verification protects against someone else fraudulently freezing or replacing a card that isn't theirs.

## Interim Access

While waiting for a replacement card, customers can access their account through:
- ATM withdrawals using a linked account PIN, where available
- Transfers through online or mobile banking
- A temporary digital card number, where supported by the account type

## What the Automated System Can and Cannot Do

The automated system can freeze a card and initiate a standard or expedited replacement once identity is verified. It cannot waive a repeat-occurrence fee outside of the stated policy or expedite shipping free of charge; such requests should be escalated to a human representative.
